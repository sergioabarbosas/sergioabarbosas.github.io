This was my first mountain trip experience. I am an amateur climber, and my goal was to climb 8 miles. I walked for four days, from National park Valle del Cocora (Salento, Quindío) to Nevado del Tolima (5,200 meters in altitude). This was a challege adventure. Fortunallly the weather conditions was incredible. The landscape was astonishing.
